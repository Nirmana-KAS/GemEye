# Privacy Policy

_Last updated 1 October 2026_

## 1. Data Collection

GemEye collects the stone photos and calibration patch photos you capture, plus your account details (name, email, phone, company, role and country). We do not collect location or contacts.

## 2. Data Storage

Photos and grading results are encrypted in transit and stored on a secured cloud server. Access is limited to your account.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not share your images, results or account details with third parties, other users or GEMCLOUD members.

## 4. Data Retention

Your grading history is kept for as long as your account is active, so you can review and compare past results.

## 5. Data Deletion

You can delete single records or all of your data from Settings. Deleted data is removed from our servers within 30 days.

## 6. Image Ownership

You keep full ownership of every photo you capture. GemEye only processes them to produce your colour grade.

## 7. AI Processing

Each photo is colour-corrected using your session calibration, then graded on our server by an EfficientNet-B0 CNN and Random Forest ensemble. Results are for guidance and borderline stones are flagged for gemologist review.

## 8. No Commercial Use

Your images are never sold, licensed or used for advertising or any purpose outside the GemEye app.
